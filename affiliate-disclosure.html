Affiliate Disclosure

Current status: no active affiliate relationship. Smart Product Picks has not yet been accepted into the Amazon Associates Program or any other affiliate program. This page will be updated the moment that changes.

Some links on this site point to Amazon product listings. At this time, those links are plain, non-tracking placeholder links (marked amazonUrl: "#" in our source data) — clicking them does not currently generate any commission for this site, because no Associates account exists yet.

We are not currently able to say "As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases," because that statement would not be true yet. We will only add that language once an approved Associates account is active and affiliate tracking tags are live on outbound links.

What will change once we join

  • Amazon links will include a tracking tag that may earn this site a commission on qualifying purchases, at no additional cost to you.
  • This page will be rewritten to include the standard required disclosure language.
  • The site-wide banner at the top of every page will be updated to reflect the live affiliate relationship.

Our editorial promise either way

Whether or not a link earns a commission, our rankings and editorial analysis are based on verified specifications and public product information — not on which retailer or product would pay us the most. See our About page for more on how we handle verified vs. unverified data.
