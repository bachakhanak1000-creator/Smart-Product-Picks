About

What Smart Product Picks is, and how it works

Smart Product Picks is an independent product research site. We publish category guides, specification comparisons, and editorial analysis of consumer tech and everyday gadgets, aimed primarily at shoppers in the United States.

Our core rule: real information only

Every product page on this site distinguishes three types of content:

  • ✓ Verified — factual specifications and details we can confirm (brand, model, published specs).
  • ◆ Editorial — our own written analysis based on verified specifications and public information. This is our opinion, not a customer's.
  • ● Not verified — fields like live price, star rating, or review count that we have not yet confirmed through an authorized source. We display this label instead of a guessed number.

Where our product data comes from today

At this stage, product names, brands, models, and technical specifications are drawn from publicly available manufacturer information. Time-sensitive fields — current price, star rating, review count, and product images — are intentionally left unverified until this site is approved for the Amazon Associates Program and can pull that data from an authorized source. We built it this way on purpose, rather than filling those fields with placeholder numbers.

What we don't do

  • We don't invent customer reviews, quotes, or star ratings.
  • We don't claim to have personally tested a product unless that's actually true and stated explicitly.
  • We don't use fake countdown timers, fake stock-scarcity messages, or invented discount percentages.
  • We don't currently have an Amazon affiliate relationship — see our affiliate disclosure for details.

Questions or corrections

If you spot outdated or incorrect information on any page, please use our contact form — we'd rather fix it than leave it wrong.
